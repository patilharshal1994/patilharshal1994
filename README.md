## About

I'm an Engineering Manager at **Salla Ads**, part of the Salla E-Commerce Platform. I lead a team of six engineers — four backend, two frontend — owning end-to-end delivery of a multi-platform advertising campaign manager used by Salla's merchant base.

I grew into management the long way: five and a half years as a senior full-stack developer, then Team Lead, then EM. That shows up in how I work — I own architecture and delivery, but I'm still in the codebase and still review diffs.

In 2025 I led the engineering workstream of Salla E-Commerce Platform's acquisition of Salla Ads — migrating the codebase, infrastructure, and delivery process into Salla's platform organisation, and was retained through the transition and appointed Engineering Manager.

**What I'm accountable for day to day**

- 🏗️ **Architecture** — the multi-network integration layer standardising Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Snapchat Ads, and YouTube APIs behind a single internal campaign model
- 👥 **People** — hiring and technical interviewing, 1:1s, performance reviews, mentoring, onboarding
- 🚚 **Delivery** — sprint planning and estimation, roadmap ownership, release management, risk and dependency tracking
- 🤝 **Stakeholders** — reporting delivery status and technical trade-offs to product and executive stakeholders; owning vendor and partner API relationships
